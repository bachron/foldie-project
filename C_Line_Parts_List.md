Round 2 (24 Oct 2026): eight parallel research passes, no 10-brand cap. Every row has a weight (g) and a price (S$). A value shown as `est.` is an estimate; the **Basis** column says how it was worked out (`page` read on a vendor page, `forum` owner/forum measurement, `listing` marketplace ad, `snippet` search snippet only, `est.` estimate). Prices are vendor/list prices converted at fixed rates (US$1=S$1.30, £1=S$1.70, €1=S$1.50), before shipping, GST and duty. Carousell, Facebook Marketplace, Shopee and Lazada could not be read directly (bot checks, not bypassed), so marketplace prices are from search snippets or are estimates.

Full links, where-to-buy and fit notes per row: `C_Line_Parts_Research.md`.
