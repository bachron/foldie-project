## Parts A: Ti stem, fork, rear triangle, headset (C Line / pre-C Line, 16"/349, rim brake, 112 mm rear / 74 mm front)

Research date: 24 Oct 2026. Extends `C_Line_Build_Options.md` sections 1-2 and `C_Line_Build_Plan.md` (existing findings reused, not redone).

**Fixed FX used for every "SGD" figure below:** 1 USD = 1.30, 1 GBP = 1.70, 1 EUR = 1.50, 1 CNY = 0.18, 1 JPY = 0.0087 (SGD). Figures are rounded. Where a Singapore storefront shows its own SGD price, that is quoted separately as "listed S$" (Fantastic4Toys SG shows about 1.305 x its USD base price).

**Tags:** *claimed* = seller/maker weight, not independently measured. *measured* = none found anywhere in this pass. "not found" = the listing page did not state it. "snippet" = seen only in a search result, page not opened. "unverified" = could not be confirmed on a page I read.

**Method / limits:** WebSearch and WebFetch, plus the built-in browser for eBay.de item pages, Shopify product JSON (Fantastic4Toys, TiBicycles, B-Spokes) and one Amazon page. Carousell was blocked by a Cloudflare "security verification" page in the browser, and I did not try to get past it, so **no live Carousell listing was read** (Carousell figures below are search snippets only). bromptuning.com (FeatherWorx) did not resolve. Amazon showed no price.

**Where to buy, Singapore column:** "SG store" means the seller ships from or sells in Singapore with SGD pricing. Fantastic4Toys (F4) is a Brompton-upgrade shop with a Singapore storefront (SGD, pickup widget shows a store; its pickup address text is placeholder, so confirm location/stock by message). B-Spokes is a Brompton authorised dealer at 83 Joo Chiat Place. All others ship from China/UK/US/EU.

Important cross-cutting finding: **Comeplay, Hexati and TiBicycles are the same Chinese factory family** (TiBicycles pages are headed "COMEPLAY / HEXATI"; the 260 g fork and 470 g triangle figures are identical on all three). Treat them as one supply source with three price points, not as independent options.

## Cross-cutting fit warnings (read first)

1. **Chain width is the biggest open question.** Brompton-spaced 7-speed cassettes from H&H state "compatible 11 speed chain only" [P]. The ACE derailleur page says cogs are 9/10-speed style [P]. The Neutron page does NOT state chain compatibility [P]. Do not buy a chain until Neutron confirms (ask: 7-speed chain or 11-speed chain?). The 7-speed chains in table 3 are standard-width (about 7.1-7.3 mm); they may be wrong for a Brompton-pitch cassette.
2. **Neutron crank and chainring are not Brompton parts.** Crank arm: 24 mm spindle, SRAM 8-bolt direct mount, "road frames with a 24 mm BB", BCD not stated, Brompton not mentioned [P]. Carbon hollow chainring: listed 50/34 and 52/36 (double, 129 g for 50/34), BCD and Brompton fit not stated [P]. Treat as non-fitting until Neutron says otherwise.
3. **Brompton-specific derailleur sets** (H&H, MiniMODs, ACE, DCCH, Suncord, thx4ride) are made to bolt to a Brompton frame's tensioner/derailleur mount (A/C/E or P/T versions differ). The owner's Ti triangle may not have that mount: ask COMEPLAY/the builder. Generic short-cage derailleurs (Shimano, microSHIFT, Sunrace) need a standard hanger or direct-mount; none of them is stated to fit a 16" bike, but all are short or super-short cage, which is the right class. No listing mentioned a "112 mm hanger"; hanger is a frame feature, not a derailleur one.
4. **Cassette top cog:** Neutron tops out at 30T. ACE derailleur max 23T [P]; MiniMODs A/C/E max 28T, tested 11-28 [P]; DCCH 11-28T, 26T recommended [P]; H&H C Line set "6-7 speed 11-28T" [P]; Sunrace RDM2T max 28T [P]; microSHIFT M21 SS max 32T per Nashbar snippet [S]. So a 30T cog is only supported by the microSHIFT M21 on paper; for the others, ask the vendor about 30T.

## Context facts that matter for the choices
- Brompton's own 2018+ caliper listing (SJS): 204 g each, 54-68 mm drop, ships with Swissstop BXP pads, pre-2018 frames need the rear fork-crown hole enlarged to 8 mm. Paul and Brompton both describe the Brompton system as short-pull (road style). So any flat-bar lever must be short-pull. Paul says its Brompton caliper is for short-pull levers only.
- Seatpost 31.8 mm and stem clamp 25.4 mm confirmed again by SJS/Kuosac/London Craftwork pages. Grip section is 22.2 mm.
- Generic road calipers have a different reach range than the Brompton 54-68 mm drop, and nothing I found confirms bolt-on fit to a Brompton fork/frame. Treat every non-Brompton-specific caliper as "reach and mounting unverified".
