## 0. Bottom line
- Best value donor: a sound **2013-2021 "classic" steel Brompton (pre-C Line) or an early C Line (2021+)**. They share the classic frame geometry, so Ti rear triangle/fork/stem kits fit. Avoid P Line and T Line as donors for Ti swap (Ti triangle listed as not fitting current P Line / T Line) [V: sjscycles].
- Titanium swap saving is about 700 g for rear triangle + fork (Brompton claim via road.cc; one owner measured ~740 g) [V]. Stock steel triangle 755 g vs Ti 461 g; steel fork 565-610 g vs Ti 265-350 g (sources disagree) [V].
- Buy a complete bike with good frame/hinges, then sell the parts you replace.

## 1. Definitions
- **Naming**: Around 2021/22 Brompton renamed: original steel range became **C Line**, entry steel became **A Line** (previously B75), then P Line (lightweight, Ti-fork/triangle-based, new design) and T Line (ultralight carbon/Ti) arrived [V: discerningcyclist, cyclechat thread]. "Pre-C Line" = anything classic before that (M/S/H/P types, 1975-2021). Note: old "P-type" (2005+ pre-Line handlebar type) is NOT the same as current "P Line" bike.
- **Timeline** (cyclechat poster) [V, forum-grade]: 2000 Mk3; 2004 Mk4 longer wheelbase; 2005 new S and P stems, titanium models; 2009 BWR hub + 2-speed derailleur, new saddle/Pentaclip; 2013 spider crankset, new brake levers, new rims; 2017 new stems/bars/shifters + Brompton Electric; 2021/22 renamed lines and P/T launched.
- **C Line** = same frame construction as A Line: hand-brazed steel main tubes, hand-brazed micro-alloy rear tubing, steel brazed fork [V]. Official-ish stock weights: C Line 11.2 kg base; Urban 11.35, Utility 11.8, Explore 12.2 kg; A Line ~11.5 kg [V: discerningcyclist]. 2024 H6L listing: 12.12 kg [V: bikeindex, one bike].
- **Model code** (e.g. M6L): letter = handlebar type (M mid-rise, S flat/low, H high/tall, P = drop-style); number = gears (1, 2, 3, 6); last letter = accessories (E none, L mudguards, R rack+mudguards) [V: Halfords]. Suffix **-X** = titanium (e.g. M6L-X) [V: eBay listing "M6L-X titanium"]. S2L = flat bar, 2-speed, mudguards. Ti variants had Ti fork+rear triangle (+ sometimes Ti hardware).
- **Serial number**: 10 digits; first four = YYMM (1903220161 = March 2019) [V: brompton.com]. Pre-2000s serials had different formats [K ⚠️]. Location of frame stamp/serial: [K ⚠️] stamped on the frame near the bottom of the main tube/left side near rear triangle hinge area, plus a sticker under the seat tube; confirm on Brompton's support page (page fetched did not state location).
- **Interchange** [K ⚠️ mostly]: classic rear triangles/forks swap across M/S/H frames of the 2000s-2021 era but hub spacing differs (3-speed SA 112 mm vs BWR 2-speed/6-speed 126 mm for rear triangle width; confirm). Ti triangle does not fit P Line / T Line [V]. Reaming of rear triangle bearings is a dealer task [V: cyclechat]. Newer shifters, bars, brake levers fit older bikes (some dealer-fit) [V: cyclechat].

## 2. Donor ranking (for Ti stem/fork/triangle rebuild)
1. **2013-2021 pre-C Line 6-speed or 3-speed rim brake complete bike** (best price/parts). Modern spider crank, newer brake levers, still classic frame. Used GBP 650-920 on eBay UK (blog) [V].
2. **Early C Line (2021-2024) M6L/H6L**: cleaner, newer hinges; costs more (UK sold GBP 850-1,090 for used Explore Mid) [V]. Best if budget allows; more resale left on stock parts.
3. **Pre-2009 / 3-speed old bikes**: cheapest, but check old standards (shorter wheelbase pre-2004, different hub width, parts obsolescence) [K ⚠️].
4. **Frame-only / frameset**: cheapest weight-wise but rarely sold; a complete bike lets you resell wheels, hub, drivetrain.
- Prefer: complete bike with **clean hinge clamps, straight fork, good rear triangle bearings**, 6-speed (Sturmey Archer hub + wheels resell well), rim-brake standard. Avoid: P Line/T Line donors, heavily rusted bikes, bikes with missing serial.
- Hardware that recoups cost: Sturmey Archer 3/6 speed hub and wheelset, steel stem and bars, Brooks/stock saddles, mudguards, racks, pedals, Brompton bags/luggage block [K ⚠️ prices unverified].

## 4. Inspection checklist
Safety/recall: Brompton recalled T Line (not C Line) built before 21 June 2024, serials 2104190001-2406070001, due to steerer clamp loosening; fix = tighten four hinge-clamp bolts to 7 Nm [V: CPSC 24-332, productsafety.gov.au]. Not applicable to C Line but a good torque habit; still check the steerer clamp on any bike.
- Frame hinges (3): unfold, clamp, wiggle; rear pivot play is a known wear issue, some owners replaced hinge bushes twice [V: forum]. Check hinge clamp plates for cracks [V: cyclechat].
- Rear triangle: pivot play/bearings; ask for reaming/bush replacement history. Check for cracks/rust near the hinge areas and around seatpost clamp [V].
- Fork: straight, no bending after crash, steerer clamp and headset smooth (pitting, notchy), paint cracking at fork crown.
- Crank: cracks around spider, bottom bracket play, pedals.
- Wheels: rim brake grooves (wear indicators), spoke tension, hub clicks in all gears (Sturmey Archer).
- Rust under paint, chipped paint at hinge, chain/sprocket wear, brake pad wear, cable fray.
- Alignment: folded/unfolded; frame sights straight, wheel tracks in line.
- Folded: latches/catches hold, magnet clicks, rear wheel/castors present.
- Ask seller: purchase date and receipt (Brompton Junction), service history, crashes, hinge replacement, who else rode it, why selling, photo of frame number/serial, sticker under saddle, registration under owner name.
- Stolen check: match frame number stamped on frame with serial and receipt; ask to see seller ID/receipt; check Brompton bike registration page (brompton.com/bike-registration) and other registries (Bike Index, police report) [V: registration link; stolen-check method otherwise ⚠️ unverified]. In Singapore, meet in public/shop; ask for proof of ownership.
- Test ride: 15+ minutes, ride no-hands briefly (tracking), brake hard, cycle all gears, fold/unfold three times, listen for hinge creak.

**Buy/pass rules of thumb (⚠️ my estimates):**
- BUY: complete bike, <= ~SGD 1,500 if pre-C, <= ~SGD 2,000 if 2021+ C Line, all condition scores <= 1, serial matches receipt, seller willing to test ride.
- PASS: cracks near hinge/fork, play in rear hinge you can't ream, P Line/T Line, missing/ground serial, no proof of ownership, price within 15% of a new base C Line at Brompton Junction.
- Budget: net donor cost should be < 40% of total build cost.

## 6. Other folding bikes (one line each, ⚠️ general knowledge)
- Tern: bigger, sturdier, 20-inch (Verge/Eclipse), heavier but good rebuild base.
- Dahon: cheap used market, aluminium frames, many upgradable.
- Birdy: aluminium with rear suspension, light, fast fold.
- Pikes (Pacific/Pikes lineup, Singapore-related brand reviews unverified): lightweight alloy foldie.
- Kwiggle: light folding bike with unusual fold; limited used supply.
