# Foldie Project: seatpost and saddle candidates, G Line and C Line / P Line

**Date:** 20 Nov 2026. **Prices:** Carousell Singapore asking prices (search results, listings not opened) unless stated; US$ at roughly 1 = S$1.3. Weights are seller or retailer claims unless stated, not weighed.
**Fit:** a standard Brompton post is 31.8 mm class (Brompton lists its carbon post at 31.66 mm) and Brompton lists the same post as fitting A, C Mk5/Mk6, P Mk1/Mk2, T and G Line. So one post type serves both bikes. C Line and P Line share the same length. The G Line is going to M. The current Hexagon Ti post is 560 mm, which is the longest the owner can use, and not all of its length is in use, so the G Line target is a post of about 560 mm or shorter. The 600 mm and extended posts are too long.
**Saddle clamp:** Brompton Pentaclip takes 7 mm round rails. Carbon-rail saddles need an adapter (Ti Parts Workshop sells one) or a carbon-rail clamp. Not verified per saddle.

> **Conflict to settle:** `C_Line_Build_Plan.md` excludes carbon seatposts because a rear child seat would clamp to the post (owner rule, T20 and C Line notes). If either bike will carry that seat, take an alloy or Ti post from the table instead. Carbon posts also have low rider-weight limits (WOOdman lists 110 kg).
> **G Line note:** the G Line already has a Hexagon Ti post (about 170-240 g, estimated, unweighed). Every carbon post below is 204-310 g, so a carbon post of the same length saves little or nothing unless the Hexagon is heavier than estimated. Weigh it first. Before ordering a shorter post, measure how much of the 560 mm is currently unused: a 540 mm post loses 20 mm of reach.

**Decision (20 Nov 2026): 540 mm fits both the G Line and the P Line.** Buy the H&H carbon 540 mm for each bike.
