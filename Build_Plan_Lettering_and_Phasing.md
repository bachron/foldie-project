## Sequencing problems to resolve before buying

1. **Axle standard.** The Priority 1 wheelset is bought before the Priority 3 fork and rear triangle. A stock G Line is 100mm front and 135mm rear quick-release; the Priority 3 parts are 100x12 and 142x12 thru-axle. A thru-axle wheelset bought in Priority 1 won't fit until Priority 3. Options: buy hubs with convertible end caps; buy a 135 QR wheelset and sell it later; or move the fork and triangle ahead of the wheelset.
2. **Drivetrain.** Priority 1 swaps the derailleur, shifter and cassette. Check what the used G Line has first (derailleur or hub gearing), because that decides whether those parts are swaps or additions, and what the stock parts will sell for.
3. **Priority 2 on the stock frame.** The Priority 2 crank, BB and chainring were picked for the T20 (M30 spindle, 110 BCD). Confirm they fit the G Line bottom bracket shell before buying.
4. **Used G Line condition.** Inspect on the trip: frame, hinges, folding mechanism, disc brake wear. Parts that are being sold later will fetch more if kept clean and complete.
