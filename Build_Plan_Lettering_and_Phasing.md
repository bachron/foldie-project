## Sequencing problems to resolve before buying

1. **Axle standard is unverified.** I earlier wrote that a stock G Line is 135mm rear quick-release. That was not confirmed: it comes only from tibicycles marketing its triangle as "converts from 135mm QR to 142mm thru-axle" (see `T20_Build_Options.md`, which says "appears to be"). A search found no spec confirming the G Line's rear spacing or axle type, and a forum post only guessed at a thru-axle. Treat the stock axle standard as unknown until checked against Brompton's G Line spec or the bike itself. Either way, the stage 1 wheelset is bought before the stage 3 fork and triangle (100x12 and 142x12 thru-axle), so check the stock standard matches before buying wheels.
2. **Drivetrain is probably not a simple swap.** Wikipedia says the G Line launched in late 2024 with 20" wheels and an 8-speed Shimano Alfine hub (internal gearing). If so, there is no derailleur, shifter or cassette to swap. Stage 1 becomes a conversion: a new freehub wheelset plus derailleur, shifter and cassette, and the stock Alfine wheel and shifter are what you sell. That may also need a different rear dropout or derailleur hanger. Confirm what the used bike has.
3. **Priority 2 on the stock frame.** The Priority 2 crank, BB and chainring were picked for the T20 (M30 spindle, 110 BCD). Confirm they fit the G Line bottom bracket shell before buying.
4. **Used G Line condition.** Inspect on the trip: frame, hinges, folding mechanism, disc brake wear. Parts that are being sold later will fetch more if kept clean and complete.
