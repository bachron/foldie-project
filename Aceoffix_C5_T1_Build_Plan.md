# Foldie Project — Aceoffix C5 / T1 Lightweight Build Plan

**Base bike:** Aceoffix **C5** (also sold under the reseller name **"T1"**): 16" / 349 aluminium (6069) trifold, carbon fork, external 5-speed derailleur, rim brakes. A Brompton T Line-style clone.
**Rider priorities:** light, practical city folder, S$ prices, Singapore sellers first, child seats possible later (**child seats and mounts excluded from every planning weight**).
**Research date:** 25 Oct 2026. Tags: `page` = read on a vendor/retailer page, `forum` = owner/forum/YouTube-comment statement, `snippet` = search snippet only, `est.` = my estimate (reason given). Rates: 1 USD = 1.30, 1 GBP = 1.70, 1 EUR = 1.50, 1 HKD = 0.17 S$.

> ⚠️ **Research quality warning.** Carousell (Cloudflare check, not bypassed), eBay (403), Alibaba (empty), Amazon (503), Shopee/Lazada (nothing indexed) were not readable. **No live Singapore used-bike listing prices were read.** The YouTube review transcripts would not load (transcript panel stayed empty), so the "Issues you should be aware of" chapter of the main review (18:31) was **not read**. ## 6. Child seats (Thule Yepp Mini front on stem, Nexxt 2 Maxi rear on post)

Nothing was found for any Aceoffix trifold carrying a child seat. **Treat as unproven.**

- **Rider limit 110 kg** (Unspokin). Rider + child + seats + luggage must stay under it. Exact budget depends on the owner's weight (not known).
- **Front, Yepp Mini on stem:** the seat clamps a stem tube of **~20-28 mm** (repo note); the Aceoffix stem tube diameter and wall thickness are **not published**. The C Line plan raises the same stem-wall concern. **Ask Aceoffix/seller in writing**, and measure the stem tube with calipers. Also the thin-tube, harsh ride, and the commenter's "stem catcher" concern make a seat load riskier.
- **Rear, Nexxt 2 Maxi on seatpost:** needs ~150 mm free post (repo note) and a 31.8 mm post: **the Aceoffix 550/580 mm post is long enough, but seat-tube play and the "likely cracking at the seatpost" comment mean this is the weakest spot.** Use an alloy post (carbon excluded by the repo rule), keep the post well inserted, and inspect the seat tube slot area regularly.
- **Pig's nose / front catch** loosened on one owner who carried heavy front loads: a front child seat is a heavy front load. Buy spare catch parts.
- Expect Aceoffix to **void/ignore** warranty claims with child seats (not stated, ask).

## 9. Risks

- Hinge/catch wear parts (pig's nose) and seat-tube play; seat-tube cracking is a commenter concern, unconfirmed.
- 118 mm rear spacing and 5-speed 11-19T drivetrain clash with the owner's 7-speed Neutron plan and Brompton 112 mm wheels.
- Naming confusion (C5 vs T1), spec drift by batch (25.4 vs 31.8 bars, Kojak vs Urban tyres).
- Warranty: new-bike terms unknown; Singapore after-sales depends on the reseller (Liaoge/Unspokin sold out).
- Ride harshness (reviewer).
- Import: GST 9%, duty and courier terms unverified; HK/China shipping time.
- 110 kg limit with children.

## 10. Open decisions / questions to ask sellers

- [ ] Factory model name: C5 or "T1"? any C5 Pro/Plus? build date and batch
- [ ] Bar clamp (25.4 or 31.8), stem tube diameter and wall, stem catch type (bearing or TPU) and spare supply
- [ ] **Rear dropout spacing (112 or 118)**, freehub body (HG?), cassette spline, derailleur cage capacity, max cog
- [ ] Chainring BCD and crank spindle; can the 50T be swapped
- [ ] Seat tube inner diameter, post diameter, sleeve part number; Brompton sleeve compatibility
- [ ] Stock weight of bike with pedals, with/without easy wheels, and frame/fork weights
- [ ] Rider weight limit in writing, child-seat policy, warranty length and who honours it in Singapore
- [ ] Brompton bag-block compatibility, rack and mudguard availability
- [ ] Price incl. delivery and GST; sold out status (Liaoge, Unspokin); spare catch/hinge pin kit
- [ ] Which tier? practical commuter weight target

**Buy / pass checklist**
- Buy: seller confirms 110 kg+, spare catch kit, written 6+ month warranty, stock weight in photos, price near S$1,360-1,640.
- Pass: no model code, 25.4/31.8 unclear, 118 mm rear unexplained, no after-sales in Singapore, carbon rims, price near S$1,950 with no extras.
