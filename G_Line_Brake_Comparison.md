# G Line brakes: UltraLevers 3S setup vs Juin hydraulic

**Date:** 26 Nov 2026, updated 27 Nov 2026. **Bought:** Extralite UltraLevers 3S (levers). **Tools:** Firecrawl.
Weights exclude rotors (160mm 6-bolt in every case).

## Levers
UltraLevers 3S: 40g on [Extralite's page](https://www.extralite.com/products/901) (read as the pair; earlier repo note 41g). The [manual](https://admin-new.extralite.com/docs/docs_68247addcad1b.pdf) says caliper and cantilever brakes (short pull). They have no master cylinder, so they cannot drive a full hydraulic caliper.

## Lewis (all models opened here are full hydraulic)
No cable-actuated model was seen in the [product list](https://www.lewisbike.com/products); not every product page was opened.

- **R2:** flat mount 34mm, 2 x 21mm titanium pistons, one-piece 7075 body, mineral oil or DOT 5.1 version (must match the lever; not interchangeable). Caliper only. US$195 each ([Lewis](https://www.lewisbike.com/products/flat-mount-rod-bike-caliper-r2)); US$204 and sold out at [Ti-Parts](https://tipartstitanium.com/products/lewis-r2-ultralight-flat-mount-monoblock-caliper), which has 2 reviews (one reports a missing manual and bleed nipple).
- **BB2 SL/SLR:** flat mount road caliper, mineral or DOT; maker says large piston clearance for compatibility with most levers. US$155 SLR, sold out at Ti-Parts.
- **[LV-BB](https://www.lewisbike.com/products/disc-brakes-lv-bb):** complete flat mount brake, 70g lever + 97g caliper = 167g without hose. For BMX, XC, gravel. Priced and specified as a single rear brake (US$209, 165cm hose); a front is not listed.
- **[LV-S2](https://www.lewisbike.com/products/disc-brakes-lv2):** XC/trial set, 300g pair without hose and pads (the same page also says 318g without hose), post mount, US$289 pair.
- Most published reviews found are of Lewis MTB four-piston models, not R2/BB2.

| Lewis setup | Published pair (g) | Complete, my estimate (g) | vs R1 + 3S ~504 |
|---|---|---|---|
| R2 + LV-BB levers (mix, not sold as a set; compatibility unconfirmed) | 266 | ~390 | ~114 lighter |
| BB2 SLR + LV-BB levers (mix, same caveat) | 277 | ~401 | ~103 lighter |
| LV-BB x 2 | 334 | ~424 | ~80 lighter |
| LV-S2 + 2 post adapters | 300 | ~422 | ~82 lighter |

Estimates add ~90g hoses and fluid; +~35g pads and fittings for the R2/BB2 mixes and for pads on LV-S2 (LV-BB: pads not stated, none added); +~32g adapters for LV-S2.

## Current Juin hydraulic (likely JF401, model unconfirmed)
- [Chapter 2 Cycle](https://www.chapter2cycle.sg/products/juin-jf401-brake-setflat-mount-black) lists caliper 82g, lever 98g. The page says "post mount" in places and flat mount in others, and 82g is very low for a 4-piston caliper. **Treat as unverified.** Face value: 360g a pair + hoses/fluid ~90g (est.) = ~450g.
- Repo estimate: 450-600g (the bike's estimates ran about 17% high).
- So the R1 + 3S setup (~504g est.) shows **no proven weight saving**. Weigh the Juin set when removed.

## Check before fitting
- Box says **3S**. The plain UltraLevers 3 is for V-brakes (long pull).
- Flat or post mount on the G Line's current fork and rear, and on the Silverrock set.
- R1 mount type; the mini velo needs its flat-bar conversion before it can take the Juin hydraulic set.
- `T20_Build_Options.md` and `T20_Build_Plan.md` pair the long-pull UltraLevers 3 with short-pull calipers: that pairing is wrong (not edited here).
