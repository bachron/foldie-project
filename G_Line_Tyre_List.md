# Foldie Project: Brompton G Line tyre list

**Date:** 18 Nov 2026. **Chosen (18 Nov 2026): Continental Contact Urban 50-406.** **Size:** 20" / 406mm bead seat, SMC carbon rims, G Line frame designed for 54-406.
**Current tyres:** Schwalbe G-One 54-406 (G-One Allround, the stock G Line tyre). Weight is not published in anything I found; I estimate ~450-525g each (900-1,050g a pair), unweighed.

## The two Continental Contact Urban sizes
Both are the same tyre family: SafetyPro Breaker puncture belt, BlackChili compound, reflective sidewall. They are two different sizes, so the choice matters.

| | Contact Urban 50-406 (20 x 2.0) | Contact Urban 55-406 (20 x 2.2), "for Brompton G Line" |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | **430g each, 860g a pair** (manufacturer figure via BIKE24, from the T20 plan) | **~560g each, ~1,120g a pair** (Clever Cycles listing) |
| Price | ~US$55 each (Performance Bike US$54.95) | US$50 each (Clever Cycles, 1 left in stock) |
| Carcass | Not checked | 3 x 60 TPI (180 TPI), E-bike rated to 50 km/h |
| Vs your current ~900-1,050g pair | about **-40 to -190g** | about **+70 to +220g** (heavier) |
| Fit | 50mm is narrower than the G Line's 54-406 design size: more clearance, slightly less volume | 55mm is 1mm over the stock 54: check clearance, especially with the carbon fork and triangle |

**Decision: the 50-406 (chosen).** It is the tyre in the T20 plan, it saves weight instead of adding it, and it keeps the Contact Urban puncture protection that suits city riding with a child. The 55-406 is the "official" G Line size and the more comfortable of the two, but it costs ~260g a pair over the 50-406 for 5mm of width.

## Checks before buying
1. **Rim width:** SMC Plume DW2 comes in C22-25 and T22-30 rim versions. Ask which you have; 50-406 and 55-406 both work on typical 22-30mm inner widths, but confirm with SMC.
2. **Clearance:** the Silverrock carbon fork and triangle you chose may have a different tyre clearance from the steel parts. Ask Chris Yeo the maximum tyre width, especially for the 55-406.
3. **Tubes:** you run tubes. Tube weight (~90-130g each) is not in this list; TPU tubes could save roughly 80-120g a pair but I have not researched them.
4. **Pressure and tubeless:** the Contact Urban is a tube tyre. If your rims are tubeless-ready (T22-30), that is a separate option, not researched.
5. **Singapore price:** find a local seller before buying from overseas.
