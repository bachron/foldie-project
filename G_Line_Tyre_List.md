## The two Continental Contact Urban sizes
Both are the same tyre family: SafetyPro Breaker puncture belt, BlackChili compound, reflective sidewall. They are two different sizes, so the choice matters.

| | Contact Urban 50-406 (20 x 2.0) | Contact Urban 55-406 (20 x 2.2), "for Brompton G Line" |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | **430g each, 860g a pair** (manufacturer figure via BIKE24, from the T20 plan) | **~560g each, ~1,120g a pair** (Clever Cycles listing) |
| Price | ~US$55 each (Performance Bike US$54.95) | US$50 each (Clever Cycles, 1 left in stock) |
| Carcass | Not checked | 3 x 60 TPI (180 TPI), E-bike rated to 50 km/h |
| Vs your current ~900-1,050g pair | about **-40 to -190g** | about **+70 to +220g** (heavier) |
| Fit | 50mm is narrower than the G Line's 54-406 design size: more clearance, slightly less volume | 55mm is 1mm over the stock 54: check clearance, especially with the carbon fork and triangle |

**Decision (superseded 21 Nov 2026 by the 42-406; the reasoning below is the original 50-406 case).** It is the tyre in the T20 plan, it saves weight instead of adding it, and it keeps the Contact Urban puncture protection that suits city riding with a child. The 55-406 is the "official" G Line size and the more comfortable of the two, but it costs ~260g a pair over the 50-406 for 5mm of width.

## Checks before buying
1. **Rim width:** SMC Plume DW2 comes in C22-25 and T22-30 rim versions. Ask which you have; 50-406 and 55-406 both work on typical 22-30mm inner widths, but confirm with SMC.
2. **Clearance:** the Silverrock carbon fork and triangle you chose may have a different tyre clearance from the steel parts. Ask Chris Yeo the maximum tyre width, especially for the 55-406.
3. **Tubes:** you run tubes. Tube weight (~90-130g each) is not in this list; TPU tubes could save roughly 80-120g a pair but I have not researched them.
4. **Pressure and tubeless:** the Contact Urban is a tube tyre. If your rims are tubeless-ready (T22-30), that is a separate option, not researched.
5. **Singapore price:** find a local seller before buying from overseas.

## Weight effect on the build
- **Contact Urban 42-406 (chosen): 710g a pair. On the 10 kg baseline (21 Nov) the current G-One pair is rescaled to about 750-875g, so the saving is only about -40 to -165g (mid -100g). The bike estimate is about 8.5 kg overall. If the G-One pair really weighs 900g+, the saving is larger: weigh one.**
- Contact Urban 50-406 (860g pair): about +35g (mid) against the rescaled G-One, i.e. no saving.
- Contact Urban 55-406: about +145g (mid), about 10.0 kg.
These are rough because the current tyres are unweighed. Weigh one current tyre, then the numbers firm up.

## 451 option (added 25 Nov 2026): 451 rims instead of 406
451 is the other "20-inch" size (the mini velo uses it). 406 and 451 tyres are **not interchangeable**: the rim and tyre bead seat numbers must match, so this option means new wheels, not just new tyres.

**Tyres:** I did not find any **35-451** tyre. The 451 sizes I could confirm are **23-451, 25-451, 28-451 (20 x 1-1/8) and 37-451 (20 x 1-3/8)** (Michelin size chart). Schwalbe One 28-451 is 280g (Perennial Cycle) and Schwalbe One 451 is US$45 (Bike Tires Direct). Schwalbe Kojak 20 x 1.35 is **35-406**, not 451: do not mix it up. Nearest to what you asked for: **37-451**; ask a shop before ordering whether a 35-451 exists.

- **Outer diameter (my arithmetic, not a published figure):** a 35-451 would be about 451 + 2 x 35 = 521mm, close to the 54-406 at about 514mm, so frame and fork clearance should be similar in length; a 35mm tyre is narrower than 54mm, so side clearance is not the issue. Confirm with the wheel seller and Chris Yeo, because the fork and triangle are Silverrock parts.
