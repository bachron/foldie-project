## Gearing (10-36T)
Gear inches, easiest to hardest; km/h at 90 rpm in top gear. Circumferences are estimates (see the build plan).

| Ring | 54-406 tyres | Top speed (54-406) |
|---|---|---|
| 54T | 30 to 108 | 46 km/h |
| 50T | 28 to 100 | 43 km/h |
| 48T | 27 to 96 | 41 km/h |

48T is about 11% easier than 54T. With 42-406 tyres, gears are about 5% easier again (48T low about 25).
