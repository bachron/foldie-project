# 1. Titanium stem and fork

Date: 2026-10-23. SGD figures use an assumed ~1.30 SGD/USD (approximate, not looked up). Weights in grams. "⚠️ unverified" = seller claim only, a mis-rendered page, or could not be confirmed on a primary vendor page.

Research limits: web tools only. Several vendor pages blocked or failed (eBay 403/500, Amazon unreadable, tibromtacks.com DNS fail, Carousell 403, the FeatherWorx page cert error). H&H, Silverock, EasyTi/Titanium-DX stems, Aceoffix, Litepro and Bromptonic stems/forks were NOT found with primary pages. They are listed as gaps, not guessed.

### 0. Key findings in one place

1. **Fitment is the real risk, not weight.** Ti stems are sold as "S/M/H/P type". The stem's handlebar-pin lock design changed: cone/expander (earlier bikes, some 2018) versus wedge (2019 onward, some 2018) per a Brompton support article summarised on CycleChat. Sellers (TiBicycles) say "with wedge" and do not state years. A pre-C Line bike (before ~2017) is almost certainly cone/expander. Confirm which the stem uses.
2. **Stock baselines (non-official, forum):** steel stem about 810 g (M/P) to 880 g (S) to 910 g (H) (BikeForums); a 2012 CycleChat list says M 806, S 866, P 602. Steel fork about 565 g (CycleChat 2012, also quoted by TiBicycles) with other figures of 560 and 610 g.
3. **Realistic Ti stem weights are 440-560 g.** Saving is roughly 170-300 g per UXN's own table (the UXN table implies an original stem of about 680-790 g).
4. **Realistic Ti fork weights are 260-320 g** (seller claims). Saving is about 250-300 g vs steel 565 g.
5. **Price range is wide:** Ti stems about US$370-$470 (about S$480-$610). Ti forks about US$220-$352 (about S$285-$460).
6. **Overtightening risk:** Brompton ships different stem-clamp torque on titanium forks. CycleChat reports damage to Ti-fork head tubes from over-tightening the stem screw (user claim). Use a torque wrench.
7. **Headset trap:** Joseph Kuosac cheap headset (SJS) suits Brompton's own Ti fork but earlier versions did not; steerer binding reported. See section 3.
8. **No aftermarket carbon 16" Brompton fork found.** The only carbon fork is Brompton's own T Line one-piece fork/steerer, which is not sold as an upgrade fitting an old frame (see section 2).

#### Technical notes

- Handlebar clamp diameter: stock Brompton bars are 25.4 mm hold (eBay titles and TiBicycles M/H bar URL show 25.4 mm). Stem main clamp 34.9 mm only inferred from a URL. ⚠️ unverified. Quote: no vendor page gave 22.2 or 31.8. Confirm 25.4 mm with seller.
- Height variants: S, M, H, P as stock. Effective lengths per TIBROMTACK: M 375, New M 330, S 375, H 400 mm (⚠️ unverified).
- "New M" vs old M: UXN lists "new M type"; the older M is a different geometry. Confirm which one your bar and cable run suit.
- Rider weight limits: none stated on any vendor page. Brompton's own general limit is not retrieved. ⚠️ unverified. Ask sellers in writing.
- Failures: no documented aftermarket Ti stem failure found in forums searched (absence of evidence). One related note: CycleChat reports damage to the head tube of Ti forks from over-tightening the stem screw (https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/height-and-reach.263459/latest). Brompton recalled T Line (before 21 Jun 2024) for steerer clamp loosening (https://www.productsafety.gov.au/node/32158 per search snippet).
- Child seat: Thule Yepp Mini clamps to stem. Wall thickness 1.2 mm (UXN) is thin; clamp load of a child-seat bracket on a 1.2 mm GR9 tube is a concern. No vendor addresses it. Ask the seller before relying on it; consider keeping the stock steel stem for any ride with the seat.

#### Fit unknowns for every aftermarket fork (ask seller)

- Steerer length and threaded vs non-threaded (Brompton uses a threaded 1-1/8" steerer; TiBi quotes 138.5 mm).
- Whether it clears the C Line (2017+) and pre-C Line headset cup depth. Joseph Kuosac Q&A: "headset suits Brompton's Titanium fork but previous versions were not" and cup binds on steerer tube.
- Offset / rake: no page gives it. Needs to match for trail and for the fold/hook-latch clearance.
- Brake mount: rim brake needs the 6 mm rear hole (Kinetics says stock hole 6 mm must be enlarged to 8 mm for front brake on its steel fork). Confirm Ti fork brake bolt hole.
- Mudguard and front-fork hook (Brompton "front fork hook assembly" for rack/luggage block) eyelets, dynamo/hub mount.
- Crown/arm width 74 mm (rim) vs 100 mm axle (disc) for hub choice.

### 3. Headset implications

- Stock Brompton steel headset is 1-1/8" threaded, loose or caged ball bearings, 36 mm spanner (https://sjscycles.co.uk/headsets/brompton-headbearing-set-1-18-steel). Crown race 29.8 mm per SJS.
- **T Line headset is different:** Brompton's T Line headset has two angular-contact bearings, listed T Line only (https://us.brompton.com/p/654/headset-for-t-line). P/T Line also have a Chris King Inset 7 adaptor listed T/G Line (S$98 on Fantastic4Toys SG).
- **Brompton's own Ti fork** has a different steerer than the steel one (Joseph Kuosac says it suits Ti fork but earlier versions do not; stack 38 mm stated, ~48 mm in a Q&A answer; advise removing spacer; £29.99) (https://www.sjscycles.co.uk/headsets/joseph-kuosac-headset-for-bromptons-1-18-black). SJS recommends part 35038 for the binding case.
- **C Line vs pre-C:** no source gave a bearing difference. Either way, a new fork needs the crown race pressed on and checking the lower cup clears the steerer. Plan to buy the headset along with the fork. Confirm with seller whether crown race seat is 29.8 mm.
- Stem clamp torque: cone/expander design 16 Nm on steel forks, 15 Nm on titanium forks; wedge design 30 Nm (Brompton article via CycleChat). Aftermarket Ti stems may specify differently; follow the stem maker and keep the Ti fork limit in mind.

#### Confirm with the seller before ordering

1. Handlebar pin lock design: wedge or cone/expander, and the year range tested.
2. Bar clamp diameter (25.4 mm?) and stem-to-bar fit for M/H bars.
3. Stem type letter and new vs old M.
4. Tube wall thickness and any rider weight limit; whether clamping a child-seat bracket is OK.
5. Fork: steerer length, threaded 1-1/8", offset, brake-hole size, mudguard/hook eyelets, dynamo mount, rim vs disc, 74 mm vs 100 mm.
6. Headset: does a stock steel or Kuosac headset fit the steerer? Supplied crown race?
7. Welding certification, GR9 or GR5, return policy, shipping to Singapore, GST.
8. Torque value and whether Brompton's 15 Nm Ti-fork figure applies.

### 5. Recommendation

1. **Buy a Ti fork first** (best $/g). Comeplay rim-brake 260 g at US$220 or TiBi 288 g at ~US$170-$180. Get written fit confirmation for C Line or pre-C (whichever bike is bought) and plan a headset (OEM or new bearing set).
2. **Ti stem second.** Best value on paper is TiBi wedge (US$370, 460 g) or Comeplay (US$425, M 450 g). UXN (US$469) has the most complete data (wall thickness, grades, tolerance) but was sold out. Stem is poor for the child-seat plan; keep the steel stem for that use.
3. **Skip** Ti bolt sets on the main stem for value unless doing a full Ti spree (13.4 g for S$13, about US$0.7/g, actually decent per gram but small).
4. **Avoid**: EasyTi T Line fork, 20" forks, 3D-printed stems at US$660+, and unverified domains.

Tiers by USD/g:
- Tier 1 (< US$1/g): Ti forks.
- Tier 2 (US$1-$1.7/g): Comeplay and TiBi stems, UXN S and M.
- Tier 3 (> US$2/g): UXN P stem, 3D-printed stems.

### 0. Key fitment correction (important)

- The brief says "135mm QR rear". A standard Brompton rear end is **112mm OLD** (MiniMODs x Hubsmith 2-speed hub listed O.L.D. 112 mm, [SJS](https://sjscycles.co.uk/hubs-other/minimods-hubsmith-2-speed-rear-hub-blue-28-hole)). - A 135mm hub (e.g. Alfine, cassette freehub) needs a **custom rear triangle** (forum figure GBP 400-725, anecdotal, unverified, [AtoB](https://www.atob.org.uk/?p=2487)). Kinetics builds custom triangles for Alfine 11 ([Kinetics](https://www.kinetics-online.co.uk/folding-bikes/brompton/pinion-brompton-conversion/)). The owner IS getting a Ti rear triangle: this is the moment to specify 135mm (or 130) and a derailleur hanger, if they want a true 1x cassette. Question for the Ti-triangle researcher/builder.
- If the existing used bike keeps a stock 112mm rear: only Brompton hub-gear / 2-speed derailleur-style (Brompton-type) sprocket hubs fit; no cassette.

### 4. Pitfalls and lessons
- Fit conflicts: SJS answers disagreed on whether Ti triangle fits C Line (2022: yes via Superlight) vs T Line (2023: no) and current P Line (not compatible). Verify per model and year (C Line 2023+ vs pre-C hinge/rear-wheel details). https://sjscycles.co.uk/brompton-spares-accessories/brompton-titanium-rear-frame-triangle-raw-finish
- Rider weight: Brompton recommends rider+luggage <=110 kg; warranty invalid above it. https://sg.brompton.com/support/knowledge/what-is-the-recommended-maximum-rider-weight-and-the-recommended-maximum-load . Brompton frame warranty is 7 yrs (2 unregistered) and covers its own frame parts; aftermarket-part effect on warranty: ⚠️ not found, ask Brompton/dealer. Child seats add load, so budget rider + child + seat <=110 kg and avoid ultralight non-OEM parts on the seat-carrying path (⚠️ my inference).
- Durability: titanium frame cracks in welds/thin transitions are reported on a non-Brompton Ti frame (anecdotal) https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/2nd-titanium-frame-failure.277964 ; Brompton warns frames suffer fatigue gradually https://global.brompton.com/support/knowledge/how-long-should-my-frame-last . Generic Chinese Ti parts (COMEPLAY/TIBROMTACK): QA unknown, inspect welds and avoid on child-carrying bikes without a trusted source.
- Weight benefit scales with rider: Heng is 53 kg; savings matter more at low rider mass.
- Alfine-type hub removal brings durability/shifting trade-offs (Heng article); on C Line the hub is lighter already, so less to gain.
- Ultra builds (Twisted Wheels, CarbonDry) sacrifice usability: single brake/gear, no grips, parts out of production.
- Weight figures in forums are often inconsistent (steel triangle 755 vs 808 g; Heng's table sums to 8.39 kg not 8.3). Weigh your own donor parts.

### 5. Target weight recommendation (child seats excluded from planning weights)
Baseline: C Line 11.35-12.1 kg. Estimates are mine, extrapolated from the evidence above (⚠️ not measured builds).

| Tier | What | Expected weight |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | Free removals (rack, fenders if climate allows), lighter saddle, tyres, pedals, Ti fork | ~10.0-10.5 kg |
| Balanced | Above + Ti rear triangle + Ti stem + Ti seatpost/BB + carbon bar | ~9.0-9.5 kg |
| No-compromise | Balanced + carbon wheelset, lighter crank/gearing, Extralite brakes/levers, carbon fork instead of Ti | ~8.0-8.5 kg |

Recommended target: about 9 kg (balanced). 8.88 kg (WeightWeenies) shows the range is realistic; anything under ~8 kg means T Line-class parts and 5.97 kg-class builds are specialist, costly projects. For a rider also carrying child seats, keep the structural parts (fork, stem, hinge clamp, seatpost) OEM-grade or from a reputable supplier and put the weight budget into wheels, saddle, pedals and tyres.

Date: 2026-10-23. Tools: WebSearch + WebFetch only. Most weights/prices are retailer-listing figures (SJS Cycles, Clever Cycles, Brompton Kitchen, London Craftwork, Carousell), not independent scale weights. Currency: GBP/USD/SGD conversions are rough assumptions (about 1 GBP = 1.3 USD = 1.7 SGD; not looked up), so treat them as order of magnitude only. "⚠️ unverified" = not confirmed on a primary page.

### 0. Key findings (read first)

1. **Seatpost is 31.8 mm, not 33.9 mm.** The 33.9 mm figure is Tern's. Ti Parts Workshop (TPW) Ti Brompton posts are sold as 31.8 mm ([SJS TPW 550](https://www.sjscycles.co.uk/seat-posts/ti-parts-workshop-titanium-seat-pillar-for-bromptons-polished-550-x-316-mm/)); Schmolke's folding-bike carbon post comes in 31.8 and 34.9 mm ([BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/bikeradar-news-feed/schmolke-brompton-folding-bike-range)). This is the **same 31.8 mm as the G Line WOOdman GT2 post** (the task notes list 31.8), so the post diameter carries over. Whether the same 31.8 mm post physically works the same way in the Brompton frame is covered in section 5.
2. **Handlebar clamp is 25.4 mm, grip section about 22.2 mm** for S, M, H and P bars on classic stems ([London Craftwork](https://www.londoncraftwork.co.uk/Handlebars/cat4666613_4825290.aspx): S and P listed "25.4mm (grip 22.2mm)"; [SJS pre-2017 M/H bar](https://www.sjscycles.co.uk/handlebars/brompton-handlebar-for-m-type-or-h-type-254mm-clamp/): 25.4 mm clamp, about 22 mm bar). So 22.2 mm applies to grips, levers and bar-ends, and 25.4 mm applies to the stem clamp. Bar-end/lever/shifter parts must work on 22.2 mm.
3. **T Line and P Line (Advance) parts are mostly NOT C Line parts, and the 2025+ C Line 4-speed and 12-speed use a new "Advance Rear Frame" (called MK6).** Brompton's Singapore rack-kit page says the rack kit is for A Line, **C Line Mk5**, Electric C Line Mk1 and P Line Mk1, and "not compatible" with Advance Rear Frame bikes, "including the 4- and 12-speed C Line" ([sg.brompton.com](https://sg.brompton.com/p/1586/complete-rear-rack-kit-black)). Confirm which frame the used bike has (Mk5 or earlier vs 2025+ MK6) before buying racks, rear hardware or rolling wheels. TPW's Ti/carbon rack is **not** for the "advance triangle" models ([SJS](http://www.sjscycles.co.uk/carriers-racks/ti-parts-workshop-titanium-carbon-rack-280mm)).
4. **Child seats:** no Brompton-specific confirmation found anywhere. Thule Yepp Mini needs a 20-28 mm quill-style stem tube or the Ahead adapter, and nothing found confirms a Brompton stem fits. The Brompton hinged stem is non-standard. The Yepp Nexxt 2 Maxi seat-tube mount takes 28-40 mm round tubes (so a 31.8 mm post is within range), but it needs 150 mm of free tube. See section 5.
