### Chosen: Ti stem + Silverrock fork and triangle set (Chris Yeo, S$)
| Item | Quote | Est. weight change |
|---|---|---|
| Ti stem | S$980 | about -290 to -550g |
| Silverrock carbon fork + rear triangle set | S$1,580 | about -1,000 to -1,450g (triangle 467.5g with axle and accessories; fork 388g with axle; vs steel triangle 1,240-1,310g and current fork 600-900g) |

Corrected 18 Nov: the Silverrock quote is the fork **and rear triangle**, not a stem, so the two items stack.
1. The bike is size S and you need M. Confirm the Ti stem is M height and fits the Chris King headset.
2. The triangle is a 142mm thru-axle rear. Confirm your SMC rear hub is 142x12 (the chat summary left rear spacing unknown), and check the Juin rear brake mount, derailleur hanger and Juin rear block.
3. Front child seat: the T20 plan found the Ti stem is the one that meets G Line geometry for the Thule Yepp Mini mount (the carbon stem did not).
4. Carbon parts have low rider-weight limits and child seats can void the warranty.
