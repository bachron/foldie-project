# Foldie Project: 160mm disc rotor list (T20 + G Line lists merged, plus 19 more)

**Date:** 25 Nov 2026. **Mount matters:** the T20 uses **Centerlock (CL)**; the G Line takes **6-bolt**. A CL rotor does not fit the G Line and a 6-bolt rotor needs a ~20g adapter on a CL hub.
**Sources:** `T20_Build_Plan.md` §3, `T20_Build_Options.md` rotors, `G_Line_Current_Parts_List.md` row 12, Carbon-Ti, Ti-Parts weigh-in (May 2026, weights measured per rotor), Weight Weenies thread, absoluteBLACK, Modern Bike (US retailer, prices in US$, no weights shown), Fair Wheel Bikes, Google Shopping SG (25 Nov, S$).
**Weights** are at 160mm unless stated. "claimed" = maker figure; "measured" = Ti-Parts scale.
