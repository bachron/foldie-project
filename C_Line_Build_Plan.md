# Foldie Project — Brompton C Line / pre-C Line Build Plan

**Base bike:** a USED Brompton, **C Line or pre-C Line** (classic hand-brazed steel frame, 16" / 349mm wheels, rim brakes). Donor not yet chosen — candidates are being shortlisted.
**Headline rebuild (the main event):** **Titanium stem + lighter fork + titanium rear triangle.** Everything else follows the T20 / G Line build's approach: lightweight, practical for city use, easier low gears over top speed, Singapore sellers preferred.
**Child seats and their mounts are excluded from all planning weights**, same as the T20 build. They still count toward Brompton's **110kg rider + load limit** (warranty is void above it).
**Research date:** 23 Oct 2026. Full tables are in `C_Line_Build_Options.md`.

> ⚠️ **Research quality warning.** Carousell, eBay, Facebook Marketplace, BikeForums and WeightWeenies blocked the research tools. **No live Singapore listings were read**, no C Line / pre-C build with a published Ti stem + fork + triangle parts list was found, and several vendor pages gave no fitment data. Everything not read off a vendor page is tagged `⚠️ unverified`. Treat weights and prices as quote-to-confirm, not fact.

> 📌 **Different platform from the T20.** The C Line is **not** a G Line. The T20 plan's fitment decisions (100/142 thru-axle, flat-mount discs, 31.8mm bar, WOOdman G Line headset, TPW G Line rack, 140mm centre-lock rotors, 11-50T cassette) **do not carry over.** Only the *approach* does.

## 1. Headline upgrades

| Part | Stock (steel) | Titanium target | Saves | Best-value pick (as researched) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fork** | ~565g (sources say 560-610g) | **260-288g** | ~277-305g | Comeplay Ti fork 260g rim / US$220 (~S$285) or TiBiCycles 288g / US$170-180 (~S$220-235) | 🛒 Fit to confirm in writing |
| **Rear triangle** | ~755g | **461-470g** | ~285-294g | tibicycles/COMEPLAY 470g / US$340-350 (~S$440-455). Genuine Brompton Ti 461g / £570 (~S$970) is the safe-but-pricey option | 🛒 Rear spacing unknown on the vendor page |
| **Stem** | ~810g (M/P) to 910g (H) | **440-560g** | ~170-400g (Heng measured 207g) | TiBiCycles wedge 460g / US$370-400 or Comeplay M 450g / US$425; UXN Ti39 has the best data but was sold out | 🛒 Child-seat concern, see §2 |
| **Combined** | | | **~730-1,000g** | Brompton itself claims Ti fork + triangle = **~700g** lighter than steel | |

📌 **Reality check from the reference builds:** the three headline parts alone take a C Line from ~11.35-12.1kg to about **10.4-11.2kg — not under 9kg.** Getting to ~9kg needs wheels, tyres, removing the rack/fenders and lighter finishing parts too.

**Cost per gram saved (US$):** forks ~0.6-0.7 (best) · TiBi / Comeplay triangle ~1.2 · Comeplay / TiBi stems ~0.9-1.2 · UXN stems ~1.6-2.7 · genuine Brompton Ti triangle ~2.5.

### Rear spacing — decide this *before* ordering the triangle
Research found a **stock Brompton is 112mm at the rear and 74mm at the front** (not 135mm). The tibicycles/COMEPLAY page gives **no spacing, dropout, eyelet or suspension-block info.** Two paths:
1. **Stay in the Brompton system** (112mm, hub gears or Brompton-style 2-speed). Cheapest, lowest risk.
2. **Custom 135/130mm triangle + hanger for a 1x cassette.** A custom triangle was quoted anecdotally at £400-725 (⚠️ unverified) and no builder was verified. This is the only route to the T20-style 11-50T low gear.

## 2. Known risks on the headline parts

- **Stem, child seat:** the UXN stem tube is **1.2mm wall** (GR9). The Thule Yepp Mini clamps to the stem. No seller addressed whether a child-seat clamp is safe on it. **Ask in writing**, or keep the steel stem for seat-carrying rides.
- **Stem lock design changed:** older bikes use **cone/expander**, **wedge** from 2019 (some 2018 bikes either). A pre-C bike is almost certainly cone/expander. Torque: **15 Nm** on Ti forks (cone design), **30 Nm** wedge; over-tightening has damaged Ti-fork head tubes (user report).
- **Fork fit unknowns:** offset/rake, brake hole (6mm vs 8mm), mudguard/hook eyelets and crown-race seat (29.8mm) are not stated by any seller. No source confirms a **C Line vs pre-C** fork difference.
- **Headset:** buy with the fork. Cheap Joseph Kuosac headsets bind on some Ti steerers.
- **No carbon option:** no aftermarket carbon 16" fork exists for the C Line. The T Line's one-piece carbon fork has an integrated headset and does not fit.
- **Rear triangle fit conflict:** SJS gave conflicting replies on which models the genuine Ti triangle fits. It is **not** for P Line or T Line. Verify per model and year.
- **Ti quality:** no documented Ti Brompton triangle failures were found (absence of evidence only). Chinese Ti clone frames get mixed owner reports and have no EN certification. Inspect welds; prefer eBay or another buyer-protected channel.
- **Keep the steel main frame.** A Ti main frame (US$740-1,000, weights unverified) is a bigger, riskier project.

## 3. Donor bike

- **Best value:** a sound **2013-2021 pre-C Line 6- or 3-speed rim-brake** bike. Next: an **early C Line (2021-24)** if budget allows. **Avoid P Line / T Line donors** (the Ti triangle does not fit them).
- **Naming:** C Line = the old classic steel range, renamed ~2021/22. Same frame construction, so kits fit. Model code: letter = bar (M/S/H/P), number = gears, last letter E/L/R = no mudguards / mudguards / rack. `-X` = titanium.
- **Serial:** 10 digits, first four are year then month (1903220161 = March 2019).
- **Price anchors:** UK eBay sold ~£650-1,090 for used C Lines; Singapore used prices are **not verified** (my estimates: pre-C 6-speed S$1,000-1,600; 2021+ C Line S$1,600-2,300 ⚠️).
- **Resale offsets:** sell the replaced steel stem, fork and triangle plus the stock wheels and hub.
- Candidate scoring sheet, inspection checklist and buy/pass rules are in `C_Line_Used_Bike_Shortlist.md`. **Paste your candidates into chat and I will score them.**

## 5. Build tiers (agent estimates, not measured builds)

| Tier | What | Expected weight |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | Free removals (rack, fenders if you accept it), lighter saddle, tyres, pedals, Ti fork | ~10.0-10.5kg |
| **Balanced (recommended)** | Above + Ti triangle + Ti stem + Ti post / BB + carbon bar | **~9.0-9.5kg** (target ~9kg) |
| No-compromise | Balanced + carbon wheels, lighter crank / gearing, Extralite brakes / levers | ~8.0-8.5kg |

References: WeightWeenies C Line at 8.88kg on a stock frame (parts list unseen); Steven Heng G Line 8.3kg (53kg rider); Twisted Wheels custom T Line 5.97kg (specialist, single-purpose); stock T Line ~7.5kg. Heng's table sums to 8.39kg, not 8.3kg: forum figures are inconsistent, so **weigh your own donor parts.**
