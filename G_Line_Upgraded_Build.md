## Size S → M conversion
- G Line has one frame; S/M/L differ only by stem height and seatpost (M = longer seatpost).
- Needs: M stem (Ti preferred), longer Ti seatpost, possibly a longer front brake hose (hoses can be shortened, not lengthened). AXS is wireless so no shift-cable issue.
- Cycling UK's 177cm tester found M too big: test ride S and M.
- COMEPLAY Ti G Line M stem: no public price, estimate S$550-750. Confirm M geometry, weight, and Chris King headset / stem catch compatibility (Asri has COMEPLAY on WhatsApp).
- COMEPLAY Ti G Line rear triangle: US$520, 571g vs stock 1,310g. Dropout spacing listed inconsistently (142 / 135mm): confirm.
- Reference: CyclingAbout's lightest G Line (9.02kg) used a Ti rear triangle (~510g saving) + Silverock Mini Bro carbon fork.

## Brake swap plan (G Line ↔ mini velo)
- Move the G Line's Juin 4-pot hydraulic set (calipers, levers, hoses) to the mini velo, only after its flat-bar conversion; check flat vs post mount.
- Put the mini velo's Juin R1 hybrids on the G Line with **Extralite UltraLevers 3S** (short-pull, 41g/pair, 22.2mm clamp). Not the UltraLevers 3 (long-pull, mushy with R1). No meaningful 2025 vs 2026 3S difference found.
- Hoses can be shortened (cut square, new olive + insert, re-bleed with mineral oil; shop ~S$30-60).
- Both bikes use 160mm 6-bolt rotors, so they are interchangeable. Re-bed or fit new pads, check thickness and pad compound (resin only), 4-pot pad coverage, T25 ~6Nm.
- Juin Tech: reputable, strong on hybrid brakes; full hydraulic less proven; local spares harder.

## Wheelset swap with mini velo: not recommended
- 406+54mm ≈ 514mm vs 451+28mm ≈ 507mm height is fine, but 54mm tyres almost certainly won't clear the mini velo frame/fork.
- Axles: SMC front 12x100 thru-axle vs mini velo QR; check rear spacing.
- Drivetrain: SRAM XDR 12-speed vs Shimano HG 11-speed are incompatible; Force AXS cannot be set to 11-speed.
- Fix if ever needed: swap freehub bodies (RHET HG 11-speed road body ~S$40-150; mini velo hub would need an XDR body). Bodies only fit their own hub model.
